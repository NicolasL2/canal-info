Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

About this app

Canal Automatico is a personal tool used only by its owner to upload videos to the owner's own YouTube channel. It is not available to other users.

What data the app accesses

The app uses the YouTube Data API with the permission to upload videos. It uses that permission only to upload videos, titles, descriptions and tags to the YouTube channel of the account that authorized it.

What data the app collects, stores or shares

The app does not collect, sell, share or disclose any personal data of any person. It does not read your YouTube data beyond what is needed to upload a video. The only credential stored is an authorization token, kept as a private secret inside the owner's own private account and never shared with anyone.

YouTube API Services

This app uses YouTube API Services. By using it you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of your data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

Revoking access

You can revoke this app's access to your Google account at any time at https://security.google.com/settings/security/permissions.
